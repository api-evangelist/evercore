Evercore announced today that Philip von Malsen-Plessen has joined the firm as a senior managing director in its investment banking business. He will be based in Frankfurt, further strengthening Evercore’s presence in the DACH region, as the firm continues to deliver on its long-term growth ambitions for Europe.
